Here are five questions I, as an Independent Director, ask in nearly every board meeting, and why management rarely asks them on their own.

**Q1: "What problem are we solving for the third time this year?"**
Surfaces recurring issues management has normalised.

**Q2: "If our ERP showed this risk six months ago, why didn't we act?"**
Tests the system-to-decision linkage.

**Q3: "What is the one number that, if it moves 10%, breaks our plan?"**
Forces sensitivity thinking.

**Q4: "Who in this room would lose sleep if this decision went wrong?"**
Tests true ownership.

**Q5: "What are we not discussing today that we should be?"**
Surfaces the silence.

A board's value is not in the answers it produces. It's in the questions it dares to ask.
