Boards love to blame the market.

"Demand was soft."

"Customers delayed orders."

"Competition was aggressive on pricing."

But strip the story back, and the chain often looks like this:

1. Finance delays the supplier advance (cash tight).
2. Procurement delays raw material (no advance).
3. Production misses the schedule (no material).
4. Marketing loses the order (no stock).
5. The Board records "soft market" in the minutes.

The market didn't fail you. Your cash conversion cycle did.

In manufacturing, Days Inventory plus Days Receivable minus Days Payable is not a finance metric. It is the single most important governance metric a board can monitor.

Because by the time it shows up in revenue, it is already 90 days too late to fix.
